Sightseeing

Sights you can visit from Mago8.

  • 醒ヶ井宿（醒井宿） / Samegai-juku

    A post town on the old Nakasendo road, with the clear Jizo River running through the town.

  • 伊吹山 / Mt. Ibuki

    The highest mountain in Shiga Prefecture (1,377 m).

  • ローザンベリー多和田 / Rozanberi Tawada

    A garden attraction in Maibara.

  • 黒壁スクエア（長浜） / Kurokabe Square, Nagahama

    An area in Nagahama known for its old townscape and glass crafts.

  • 彦根城 / Hikone Castle

    A castle whose main keep is designated a National Treasure.
